# SOVRA.NEXUS: Early Adopter Evaluation Protocol

**Distribution:** Allocation 0001 - 1000  
**Subject:** Technical Evaluation and Preferred Pricing Terms  

Organizations participating in the SOVRA.NEXUS early adopter program receive preferred introductory pricing. In exchange, participation requires a technical evaluation of the deployment experience, architecture, and operational results. 

This document outlines the expectations for that evaluation. 

## 1. The Objective (What is required)
We require a structured public review of your deployment of the SOVRA.NEXUS node. The evaluation must cover three core areas:
- **Deployment Efficiency:** The friction encountered when integrating the Docker environment into your existing infrastructure.
- **Cognitive Containment:** The performance of the Semantic Firewall and Dual-Lock Trap Tokens during your internal red-team testing.
- **Operational Utility:** The latency and success rate of the outbound Motor Cortex (API proxies).

## 2. The Rationale (Why this is required)
The architecture of SOVRA.NEXUS introduces a paradigm shift: moving from cloud-reliant API gateways to localized, sovereign digital organisms. To establish a cross-platform reproducibility standard, we rely on elite engineering teams across diverse hardware profiles to validate our benchmarks. Your evaluation provides the empirical data necessary to harden the system for global enterprise deployment.

## 3. The Outcome (The effect of your action)
By submitting this technical evaluation, you secure your preferred pricing tier in perpetuity for the lifetime of the deployed node. Furthermore, your data directly informs the next iteration of the Axiom Fuzzer, ensuring that the subconscious immune system protecting your specific node remains ahead of emerging prompt injection vectors.

## 4. Evaluation Formatting (Room to Maneuver)
We do not prescribe a rigid template. You may publish this review as a technical blog post, a GitHub Gist, or a whitepaper. We ask only that you focus on exact claims, documented methods, and clearly scoped deployment parameters. Validate the benchmarks on your own terms.
